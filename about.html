MY STORY

From the C-Suite
to the AI Frontier

I spent 20 years as a Chief Operating Officer — building systems, managing teams, hitting margins, and scaling businesses from the inside. I've sat in every meeting where someone said "we need to cut costs" or "we're behind on this." I know what operational pressure feels like from the inside.

The companies I then helped? All SMBs. All with the same struggles — too many manual processes, disconnected software, teams stretched thin. They needed someone who understood the business problem, not just the technology answer. That's my edge.
