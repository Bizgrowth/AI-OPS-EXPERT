How does the 90-day guarantee work? +
Simple: if your AI implementations don't produce measurable ROI within 90 days of going live — whether that's time saved, costs reduced, or revenue generated — I refund 100% of my fee. We define success metrics together upfront, so there's no ambiguity. I've never had to issue a refund.

Absolutely not. I've built AI systems for restaurant owners, contractors, spa managers — none of them were technical. I handle every technical aspect from start to finish. You just need to understand your business well (which you already do) and be willing to adopt new workflows.
How long does a typical engagement take? +
The AI Readiness Assessment takes 1–2 weeks. A full automation build is typically 2–4 weeks. Full AI Operations deployment runs 6–10 weeks. Everything is planned upfront with clear milestones so you always know exactly where we stand.

Yes — almost certainly. I specialize in connecting disparate software systems using integration platforms like n8n, Make, and Zapier.
